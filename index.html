Our Story

Sweetening Lives
Since 1985

SugarDream began in a small kitchen in Jaipur, where our founder Priya Sharma spent hours perfecting her grandmother's secret mithai recipes. What started as gifts for neighbours soon became the most beloved sweet shop in town.

Today, we blend age-old Indian traditions with contemporary confectionery techniques to create sweets that are not just delicious — they're unforgettable experiences.
